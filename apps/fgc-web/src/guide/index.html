Executive summary

What the app is for

FGC-Ops is the operations app for the FIRST Global Challenge. It runs on web, Android and iOS, in several languages, with light and dark themes. It needs an internet connection: nothing is saved until the app confirms it.

  • Judging — panels, teams and judges' observations, with live progress.
  • Filming — shot list, Step & Repeat coverage and a team map.
  • Pager — send a message to a team's mentor and read the reply.
  • Administration — who has access, the shared team register and mentor codes.

What you see depends on the roles an administrator gave you:

Everyone

Signing in and getting around

  1. Open the app and type your email, then choose Send sign-in email.
  2. Open the link in the email, or type the code it contains and choose Verify code.
  3. First time only: enter your full name and save your profile.
  4. If you see “Your email is verified. Ask an administrator to enable access”, you have no role yet — ask an admin.

Mentors don't use email: type the team access code in the same field.

Header and menu

  • Language and light/dark switches sit in the header, next to your account menu.
  • The account menu has Feedback, Report a bug and Sign out.
  • The bottom bar (or side menu on wide screens) lists your modules and Useful resources.
If the connection drops while saving, the app warns you and keeps what you typed. Fix the connection and press save again.

Role

Admin

Admins control access and the shared data. Open Admin from the menu.

Grant or change access

  1. Open Current users and search for the person.
  2. Set their roles (Judge, Judge Advisor, Filmmaker, Head Referee, Admin) or remove them.

An admin can't also be a judge: keep the two apart.

Import the teams

  1. Open Import teams and choose a file (.xlsx, .csv, .txt or .json).
  2. Map each column to the app's fields (suggestions are filled in; check them).
  3. Review the preview. Nothing is saved yet. Valid and rejected rows are listed apart.
  4. Choose Confirm and import valid rows. The result lists what was imported, rejected or already registered.

Re-importing never changes teams that already exist.

Mentor codes

  1. Open Mentor codes and filter by team name or country.
  2. Choose Issue code (or Regenerate code to replace one).
  3. Copy it at once — it is shown only one time — and give it to the mentor.

A code works for 7 days. Regenerating signs out the old devices.

Role

Judge Advisor (JA)

The JA organises judging and follows progress across all panels.

Set up

  1. Add teams to Judging by picking from the imported register (you can't create new ones).
  2. Create the panels and split the teams among them. A panel can start empty, with no judges yet.
  3. Add judges, then choose each panel's leader from its judges. Without a leader nobody can mark a team as evaluated.
  4. Each judge and each team belongs to one panel at a time.

During the event

  • The dashboard shows progress: evaluated teams out of active teams.
  • You can read observations from every panel.
  • Flag a team as absent, online interview or other impediment (reason required). These are informational.
  • Withdrawn from evaluation blocks new notes and completion; give a reason. Reactivating restores the earlier status.
  • Move a pending team without notes, or a judge, to another panel. To move a leader, name a new one first.
  • Reopen a completed team if judges need to add or edit notes.

When the competition ends

Use the end-of-competition cleanup. It asks for two confirmations because deleting the judging data cannot be undone. Cancelling any step deletes nothing.

Role

Judge

Open Judging to see your panel and its teams.

  1. Pick a team from your panel.
  2. Write your observation and save it explicitly. Success is shown only after the server confirms.
  3. If saving fails, your text stays on screen. Try again.
  4. Leaving a note with unsaved changes asks whether to keep editing or discard.
  • You can read the notes of the other judges on your panel.
  • You can edit or delete only your own notes, while the team is active and pending.
  • Need the team now? Use Page this team; it opens the pager on that team.

Panel leader

Only the leader marks a team as evaluated. The app asks you to confirm every time, even with no notes. The status is shared by the whole panel. The leader or the JA can reopen it.

Role

Filmmaker

Open Filming to capture the competition one team at a time.

  1. Use Coverage to see how many teams are captured. Filter by continent, or switch between list and map.
  2. Open a team and set its status: Captured, Skipped or back to Pending. Add shot notes if useful.
  3. In the Shot list, tick shots as complete or reopen them. Search by team or shot.
  4. Add your own categories and shots; delete a shot you no longer need.
  5. Need a team in front of the camera? Use Page team.

Role

Head Referee

Open Referee (Referee notes) to read what judges wrote and add your own notes.

  1. Search by country, team or panel. The page stays empty until you search.
  2. Open a team to read every judge annotation.
  3. Write your refs note and choose Save refs note. You can delete it later.

The judges of that team's panel can read your refs notes.

Role

Mentor

Mentors only receive and reply. No email is needed.

  1. On the sign-in screen, type the team access code the admin gave you.
  2. Open Team messages. Judging and filming messages are labelled.
  3. When a message asks for a response, tap one quick reply: On our way, ETA ~10 min or Can't come now.
  4. The Filming checklist shows what your team's shoot needs.

Codes last 7 days. If yours stops working, ask an admin for a new one.

Judges, JA, filmmakers

Using the pager

  1. Open the pager from your module, or from Page this team / Page team.
  2. Find the team (or change it).
  3. Choose a ready-made message or write your own (500 characters at most).
  4. Choose Send now, or schedule it in a few minutes, then Send message.
  5. Watch Message history: it shows Awaiting response or the mentor's answer. Filter by team if the list is long.

Remember

TL;DR

  • Access An admin gives roles. No role, no modules.
  • Saving Always press save. Trust a success message, not a hunch.
  • Judges Notes are yours to edit; the leader completes teams; the JA runs panels.
  • Filming Mark each team captured or skipped; page teams from the same screen.
  • Referees Search a team, read the judges' notes and add a refs note.
  • Mentors Sign in with the team code and tap a quick reply.
  • Stuck? Use Feedback or Report a bug in the account menu.
